Open-Source RFIC Design & Verification of a 28 GHz Two-Stage Class-A SiGe Power Amplifier

Designed and simulated a two-stage 28 GHz Class-A SiGe power amplifier using the IHP SG13G2 130-nm SiGe BiCMOS Open PDK and an open-source RFIC workflow based on Qucs-S, ngspice/Xyce, KLayout, and EM-aware modeling.

  • Developed a progressive ideal-passive → PDK-aware → EM-aware realization workflow.
  • Final process-aware small-signal configuration achieved approximately 17.4 dB gain at 28 GHz, with S11/S22 around −13 dB and K = 12.
  • Explored ML-assisted design-space screening and optimization; a thesis-derived manuscript has been submitted to ICCIT 2026.

Independent Research · 2026–PresentOngoing

GNN-SABLE: GNN-Guided VLSI Floorplanning with Constraint-Aware Refinement

Developing a learning-guided floorplanning framework that combines GNN-based placement guidance with B*-tree simulated annealing, legalization, and exact-evaluated refinement.

  • Investigates trade-offs among wirelength, compactness, legality, boundary, grouping, and related structural constraints.
  • Builds a rigorous experimental workflow with controlled baselines, ablations, leakage-aware data handling, legality checks, and stage-level evaluation.
  • Uses Intel Labs FloorSet/FloorSet-Lite data as the basis for reproducible evaluation.

Research Collaboration · OngoingMSCAD collaborator

Leakage-Aware RTL-to-QoR Prediction for Early Area & Timing Estimation

Working with Zakia Sultana, Ph.D. Candidate in the Electronic Design Automation Group at the MSCAD Laboratory, University of Arkansas, on pre-mapping QoR prediction directly from RTL.

  • Developing area and timing prediction using a curated RTL dataset with source provenance and duplicate/near-duplicate control.
  • Working on leakage-aware grouped evaluation, RTL structural feature engineering, and reproducible model-development pipelines.
  • Investigating size-aware prediction, explainability, extrapolation analysis, and out-of-distribution awareness.
